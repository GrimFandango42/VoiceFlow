# VoiceFlow — Development Log

## Project Overview
Voice-to-text application. Two ways to run: from source via `dev.py` with hot reload (model server + app, `.venv-gpu` for CUDA) and as the packaged `dist\VoiceFlow\VoiceFlow.exe` (single process, CUDA DLLs bundled, self-verifying build). `Ctrl+Shift` is the push-to-talk hotkey. See [docs/ARCHITECTURE.md](../docs/ARCHITECTURE.md).

*Entries from April 2026 are historical snapshots; the process and status details in them are not current.*
